About DA

DA has managed support and community operations at scale, supporting technical ecosystems with communities exceeding 80,000 members across multiple platforms and products.

Jon Williams, founder

Known online as Jonny Ringo, Jon founded DA Operations in 2021 under its original name, DAO Academy. His work combines technical support, community operations, software development, and ecosystem analytics.

He has led technical support and escalations across the Arweave and AO ecosystems, working with founders and engineering teams. Earlier roles included semiconductor equipment engineering at Intel and technical support escalation at KellyConnect for Apple iTunes.

DA Operations

Our work began in community management, where solving user problems often required learning complex products, investigating technical issues, maintaining documentation, and coordinating across teams.

Today, that experience informs both scoped technical projects and ongoing operations. We build reporting and automation, help developers get started, improve support processes, and carry technical issues through investigation and engineering follow-up.

People need someone who understands the situation and follows through. Recurring support questions also help identify gaps in documentation, confusing features, and issues that need attention from product or engineering.

Create a link to share

Optional: see which posts bring people to DA. Your regular website link already tracks visits. This tool adds labels to a link; it does not post anything or run an ad.

  1. Choose a page and where you will share it.
  2. Create and copy the link, then paste it into your post or message.
  3. Return here after people visit. Check Traffic sources, or Shared link results if you added a group name. Allow time for new visits to appear.

A group name connects related posts in your results. Spaces are fine: October Spooky Vibes becomes october_spooky_vibes in the link. Use public labels, not personal information.

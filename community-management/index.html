Support that understands your product

A busy Discord or Telegram community creates work beyond moderation. Questions need answers, recurring problems need investigation, and your engineering team needs enough context to act. We can take on that work, support your existing team, or help you set it up.

Community and customer support

  • Answer member questions and help people use your product.
  • Manage moderation, channel organization, and community rules.
  • Set up Discord and Telegram moderation tools and ticketing systems.

Technical support and developer onboarding

  • Learn your product, documentation, and common support issues.
  • Help developers work through setup and integration questions.
  • Investigate issues and give engineers clear reports with the context they need.

Community reporting and social profiles

  • Gather data on recurring questions, community activity, and emerging concerns.
  • Create and automate ecosystem statistics dashboards with charts and statistical comparisons for better context.

Documentation and follow-through

  • Repeated questions become help articles, onboarding improvements, or even UI suggestions.
  • Coordinate escalations from both users and partners with engineering, public relations, and executive leadership.
  • Suggest bug fixes and PRs after appropriate research and data collection supporting user needs.

Common questions

Is this an agency? Will I get a random, low-quality mod?

DA Operations is a management organization that specializes in community growth and support. CEO Jon Williams personally onboards and works with each new partner.

Which platforms can you support?

We have experience with Discord, Telegram, X (Twitter), Farcaster, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook (Meta), just to name a few. We also manage social profiles, email, live chat, and help desks. We take pride in being flexible and learning fast, regularly gaining experience on new platforms.

What coverage can we arrange?

We'll discuss the hours and time zones your community needs, expected response times, peak traffic windows, and what works best to get your support up and running.

Aren't sure about these details? No sweat! We run 7-day support sprints to learn your community's needs and map out what works for community growth and support so you don't have to.

How much does community management cost?

Ongoing support is quoted around the number of platforms, workload type, regional coverage, technical depth, reporting needs, and responsibilities involved. We start with our 7-day support sprint at $149 to better understand those needs and help avoid unnecessary costs.

Can we start with a smaller test project or batch before ongoing support?

Yes. Our 7-day support sprint starts with choosing a focused area of support together. During the week, we get to know your product and members, help with questions, and gather data on busy periods, recurring issues, and support gaps.

We finish by reviewing what we learned with you and recommending coverage, reporting, and practical improvements. Once we have a better understanding of your support needs, we can put together a proposed partnership agreement for community and product support.

How do technical issues reach engineering?

We gather the symptoms, impact, and steps to reproduce, then use the escalation route agreed with your engineers. We keep track of ownership and follow up with the person who raised the issue. Our technical support escalation guide shows what that handoff should include.

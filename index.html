Start with one problem.

A small, defined task can be a useful place to start. Examples we can scope with you:

  • Repair a reporting script. One existing script and one data source; a corrected script checked against an agreed sample.
  • Automate a repetitive data task. One input format and one repeatable output; a reusable script with instructions.
  • Investigate an application issue. One reproducible problem; written findings and recommended next steps. A fix is separate unless agreed.

Examples for discussion, not fixed-price packages. Suitability and price depend on the agreed scope.

The Eye: Arweave & AO analytics

Activity across two technical ecosystems was spread across different sources. We built The Eye to collect that data and make it easier to explore in one dashboard.

Users can compare network and developer activity, inspect transaction trends, and explore marketplace and gaming statistics without querying each source themselves.

The Eye was built under our former name, DAO Academy. We now operate as DA Operations.

Technical details

The application is hosted on Arweave, queries AO processes, and combines those results with indexing and reporting services. These are technologies used by the ecosystems it monitors.

How we work

  1. 1. Understand

    Tell us what's happening and what you need. We learn the product, investigate the issue, and identify a useful starting point.

  2. 2. Agree the scope

    We agree on deliverables, price, and expectations before work begins. Standalone projects don't require a long-term commitment.

  3. 3. Do the work

    We fix, build, investigate, or operate. AI assists our research and workflows; experienced people stay responsible for the work.

  4. 4. Follow through

    We review the agreed deliverables with you, explain what changed, and document the next steps or any issues that need further attention.

How we work

  1. 1. Understand

    Tell us what's happening and what you need. We learn the product, investigate the issue, and identify a useful starting point.

  2. 2. Agree the scope

    We agree on deliverables, price, and expectations before work begins. Standalone projects don't require a long-term commitment.

  3. 3. Do the work

    We fix, build, investigate, or operate. AI assists our research and workflows; experienced people stay responsible for the work.

  4. 4. Follow through

    We review the agreed deliverables with you, explain what changed, and document the next steps or any issues that need further attention.

The Eye: Arweave & AO analytics

We built The Eye to collect and visualize activity across the Arweave and AO ecosystems. It brings network activity, developer metrics, transaction analytics, marketplace statistics, and gaming data into one accessible interface.

Hosted on Arweave, the application queries AO processes and combines ecosystem data with indexing and reporting services. It uses the technology of the ecosystem it monitors.

For custom requests, we investigate the underlying technology, work directly with the available data, and build a solution around the actual problem.

Analytics privacy

How we measure activity on this website.

What we collect

We measure page visits, navigation and contact-button clicks, scroll milestones, visits with at least 15 seconds of visible page time, and clicks opening our Calendly appointment page. These clicks do not confirm a booking. Historical contact-form events remain in the analytics. Events include a random session identifier, time, page name, screen-size category, referring website hostname, and promotion labels from tagged links.

We do not include names, email addresses, company details, message text, IP addresses, full browser identifiers, or complete URLs in analytics events. Appointment clicks may include one of four fixed service categories, so we can understand which services interest visitors. Booking information is handled separately by Calendly.

Public, permanent storage

Analytics events are sent to an Arweave upload service and stored on the public Arweave network. Published events can be read by anyone and cannot be removed. The analytics dashboard can publish weekly snapshots of these same public records to speed up future loads. Upload and query services receive ordinary network requests, including connection information; this site does not control their logs.

Browser storage

Your browser tab holds a random session identifier and a temporary signing key. Sessions expire after 30 minutes without tracked activity. Unsent events are retried for up to 24 hours while the tab remains available. Closing the tab clears this tab storage. An opt-out preference is saved in this browser until you clear it. The dashboard also caches up to 120 days of public analytics locally, within a 3 MiB limit. Clearing browser storage removes these local copies.

Your choice

We honor Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. You can also stop future analytics collection and shared-cache uploads in this browser below. This clears pending events in this tab; it cannot retract events already sent. Preferences are specific to the browser and site address you use.

Appointments and inquiries

Contact buttons open our Calendly page, where you can choose an appointment and provide your booking details. DA Operations uses those details to arrange and respond to your consultation. Booking details are not included in the site's public analytics.

See Calendly's privacy notice for information about its service. For questions about information you have sent to DA, email jonny@daops.net.
